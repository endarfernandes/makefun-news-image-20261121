## HERO — NATO rallies for Poland

- **Chip:** WAR — POLAND
- **Headline:** NATO RALLIES FOR POLAND
- **Deck:** Rutte flies to Warsaw as Poland seeks more NATO air
  defences after Russian missiles fell on its soil. A Swedish pilot
  was cleared to shoot — but held fire in bad weather.
- **Breaking strip:** Rutte in Warsaw as Poland seeks NATO cover
- **Facts:** NATO secretary general Mark Rutte is due in Warsaw on
  Saturday 21 Nov. Poland formally asked NATO for more air defences
  under Operation Eastern Sentry — AWACS aircraft and F-35 jets —
  after Russian missiles crossed into Polish territory earlier in the
  week. A Swedish Gripen pilot was cleared to shoot down a Russian
  Kh-101 cruise missile but held fire because of bad weather. Poland
  did NOT formally invoke Article 4.
- **Why hero:** the biggest story of the day — the top headline
  across Reuters, AP, ISW and European press on 21 Nov; a NATO
  ally asking for more allied air power after missiles landed on
  its soil.
- **Sources:** Reuters; AP; ISW (Institute for the Study of War);
  europesays / sofx reporting of the Reuters/AP wires.

## 6 — Cancer pill: tumours vanish

- **Chip:** HEALTH
- **Headline:** CANCER PILL: TUMOURS VANISH
- **Deck:** Daraxonrasib plus chemo made tumours undetectable in 1
  in 12 patients; 60.7% shrank or vanished.
- **Facts:** Daraxonrasib (a RAS inhibitor) combined with
  chemotherapy made tumours shrink or disappear in 60.7% of
  patients, undetectable in 1 in 12 (8.2%), with disease controlled
  in 90.2%. Early trial, 61 patients. Presented 20 Nov in
  Barcelona at the EORTC-NCI-AACR symposium and reported worldwide
  on 21 Nov.
- **Date anchor:** presented 20 Nov, reported 21 Nov — the deck
  makes no date claim.
- **Sources:** The Guardian; New York Post; Medindia.
