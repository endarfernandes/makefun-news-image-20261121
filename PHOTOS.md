# PHOTO SOURCES & LICENCES — news-image-20261121

Every photograph is a real photograph. No AI-generated images. No
vector illustrations. No Reuters / AP / AFP / Getty images — those
are rights-managed and were treated as unusable. Every licence
below was read from the file's Wikimedia Commons `extmetadata`
(LicenseShortName) at download time, 21 Nov 2026. All files were
downloaded at 1280 px wide from Commons thumbnails.

Attribution: CC BY / CC BY-SA licences require attribution. The
image itself carries a compact credit line in the footer ("Photos:
Wikimedia Commons (CC / public domain) · NOAA · White House — see
PHOTOS.md"); full credits are this file, bundled in the repo.

Three photos are reused from the 20261120 R3 edition (trump.jpg,
akvan.jpg, antalya.jpg) — the user's own earlier entry, so reuse
is permitted; their SHA-256s were re-verified after copying and
match the R3 values exactly.

Honesty note per photo: a photo is marked EVENT (it shows the
actual event), PERSON (official portrait of the person in the
story), LOCATION (the place/institution in the story, photographed
earlier), or SUBJECT (the subject/commodity of the story). Nothing
is presented as the 21 Nov event itself unless it is.
